# Frozen Bottle SEO Growth Case Study

A 12-month SEO case study on how **Frozen Bottle** expanded organic discovery by connecting campaign demand, product search intent, programmatic SEO, and continuous content optimisation.

## Frozen Bottle

### Objective
Build a scalable organic acquisition engine by connecting marketing activity with search behaviour, expanding keyword coverage, and creating more relevant organic entry points.

**Period:** May 2024 → May 2025

### Strategy
**Campaign-led SEO · Product SEO · Programmatic SEO · Content Strategy · Search Intent · Continuous Optimisation**

### Execution
- Connected campaigns and promotions with emerging search demand to identify relevant SEO opportunities
- Built product and offer-related discovery around **transactional and navigational** search intent
- Published **500+ programmatic articles and city-focused pages** using a scalable page framework
- Published **2 new blog posts per week** and refreshed **1 existing article per week**
- Used campaign and search signals to decide what to publish, refresh, or expand
- Expanded visibility across **AI Overviews, People Also Ask, featured snippets, and branded search**

### Results
- **32,039 → 147,452** monthly organic visits
- **4.6×** reported organic traffic growth
- **24,399 → 72,095** monthly branded searches
- **+195%** reported branded search growth
- **500+** programmatic articles and city pages
- **10–12** reported organic leads per day from other cities
- **3–4×** reported annual search visibility increase

*Metrics are reported in the original case-study presentation and have not been independently audited in this write-up.*

## How I Approach SEO

**Understand the demand → Map search intent → Build relevant coverage → Measure outcomes → Iterate**

The focus is not just publishing more pages. It is building a repeatable system where campaign activity and search behaviour inform content, product discovery, and the next optimisation decision.
